Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

Important: This privacy policy is provided for transparency. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. The owner of this service should review and customize this document before relying on it for legal compliance.

What We Collect

We do not collect, store, or log the email text you submit for analysis.

When you submit an email for analysis:

  • The subject and body text are sent to our server over an encrypted HTTPS connection.
  • The text is processed in memory to generate the analysis result.
  • The text is discarded immediately after the response is sent. It is not written to any database, file, or log.
  • We do not use your submitted text for model training or any other purpose.

What the Hosting Platform May Log

SpamGuard AI is hosted on Render.com. Like most hosting platforms, Render may automatically log certain operational data that we cannot prevent:

  • IP addresses in request logs (standard web server behavior)
  • Request timestamps and HTTP status codes
  • User-Agent strings from your browser

We do not control Render's logging practices. See Render's Privacy Policy for details.

Our Application Logs

Our application logs contain only:

  • Timestamp
  • API route accessed
  • HTTP status code
  • Response latency
  • A random request ID (not linked to any user identity)

Our logs never contain email subject lines, body text, or analysis results.

Cookies

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics services, or any form of user tracking.

Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No CDN scripts are loaded. All assets are served from our own domain.

Data Retention

We retain no user data. The hosting platform's operational logs are subject to their own retention policies.

Children's Privacy

This service is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the Feedback page.
