Important Limitations

Transparency is a core value — here is what this tool cannot do

Not a Safety Guarantee

This tool provides a best-effort analysis. It does not guarantee an email is safe or malicious. Always exercise caution.

Text Analysis Only

We analyze subject and body text only. We cannot check headers, attachments, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, or sender reputation.

Training Data Age

The model is trained on the Apache SpamAssassin corpus (early 2000s). It may not recognize the latest phishing techniques.

Free-Tier Hosting

Hosted on a free tier with limited resources. The server may take up to a minute to wake up after periods of inactivity.
